# Apex — Executive Business Intelligence & Revenue Command Center

Apex is a high-performance, responsive executive sales and revenue intelligence dashboard engineered for enterprise commerce operations. It provides real-time tracking of revenue, order volume, average order value (AOV), net margins, regional distribution, channel contribution, and automated business performance insights.

## Live Deployment

- **GitHub Pages Live URL**: [https://joshuahan07.github.io/tastelabsuxprobe/sites/dashboard/antigravity/](https://joshuahan07.github.io/tastelabsuxprobe/sites/dashboard/antigravity/)

## Key Architectural & Functional Highlights

1. **Executive KPI Metric Ribbon**
   - **Gross Revenue**: Period totals with comparative delta percentages vs. prior period and micro sparklines.
   - **Total Orders**: Volume tracking with fulfillment pacing indicators.
   - **Average Order Value (AOV)**: Basket size analytics and items per order.
   - **Net Profit & Margin**: Cost of goods sold (COGS) tracking with margin percentages.
   - **Conversion Rate**: Multi-touch funnel efficiency metrics.
   - **Return & Refund Rate**: Operational fulfillment health risk tracking.

2. **Executive Performance Summary (Automated AI Insights Engine)**
   - Dynamic natural-language briefing generated on the fly based on selected timeframe, categories, and regions.
   - **Business Health Score (0-100)**: Evaluates revenue acceleration, margin health, and return velocity.
   - Strategic takeaways across Revenue Trajectory, Product Leadership, Channel Efficiency, and Operational Recommendations.
   - **Quarterly Target Pacing Bar**: Progress against a \$1.5M revenue target.

3. **Interactive Visual Analytics (Zero-Dependency Custom Vector SVGs)**
   - **Revenue & Orders Trend**: Multi-series SVG area/line chart with dual period comparison, aggregation toggles (Daily, Weekly, Monthly), and crosshair tracking tooltips.
   - **Accessible Data Table Toggle**: One-click switch between visual chart and accessible data table.
   - **Channel Mix Donut Chart**: Proportional breakdown across Direct E-Commerce, B2B Wholesale, Marketplace, Retail Stores, and Affiliates.
   - **Category Velocity Bars**: Visual rankings across 6 major product verticals.
   - **Regional Expansion Matrix**: Global revenue share and YoY acceleration for North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and Middle East & Africa.
   - **Order Fulfillment Distribution**: Status ring and bars for Completed, Processing, Refunded, and Cancelled orders.

4. **Product Catalog Leaderboard**
   - Top-ranked SKUs with unit velocity, gross revenue, category tags, catalog revenue share, and stock health indicators.

5. **Enterprise Transaction Ledger & Order Management**
   - Instant search across Order ID, customer names, SKUs, and email addresses.
   - Status tab pills with real-time counts.
   - Column sorting across ID, timestamp, customer, status, and total amount.
   - **Detailed Receipt Modal**: View itemized receipts, payment gateway details, and change order status in real time.
   - **Transaction Simulator**: Add mock transactions that immediately recalculate all KPIs, charts, and tables.
   - Pagination controls with custom rows-per-page.

6. **Global Utilities & Accessibility**
   - **Multi-Currency Engine**: Instant recalculation across USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), and JPY (¥).
   - **Theme Engine**: Dark / Light mode toggle with system preference detection and localStorage persistence.
   - **Data Exporters**: Export active view to CSV or JSON, and formatted Print / PDF view.
   - **Mobile-First Responsive Layout**: Optimized for viewports from 320px smartphones to 4K ultra-wide monitors.
   - **WCAG AA Compliant**: Semantic HTML5 landmarks, ARIA live regions, skip links, and full keyboard navigation.
